denoiser · babylon.js

Babylon renders a noisy PBR showcase and the denoiser cleans it, either on its own GPU device with a CPU copy each way, or on Babylon's device with no copies at all.

Two ways to connect Babylon and the denoiser. CPU copy, two devices: the denoiser runs on its own GPU device, so each frame crosses on the CPU: RenderTargetTexture.readPixels() (GPU→CPU) → denoiser.denoise() → RawTexture.update() (CPU→GPU). Works on any engine, WebGL2 included, and the copies are timed below. Shared device, no copies: the denoiser runs on Babylon's own GPUDevice (engine._device: Babylon has no public getter for it yet), reads the render target's GPUTexture directly, and writes into a texture Babylon wraps with engine.wrapWebGPUTexture(). Nothing leaves the GPU. WebGPU engine only. See the custom-engines guide.
