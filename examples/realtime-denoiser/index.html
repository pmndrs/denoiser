Real-time denoiser test bed

Analytic WGSL ray tracer: 1-spp diffuse GI, specular and shadow signals with converged references, scripted stress paths and metrics, for the clean-room denoiser/realtime work.

What this is

Every frame the tracer writes the buffers of docs/research/rt-denoiser/CAPTURE-FORMAT.md: depth, viewZ, normal, roughness, motion (+ 2.5D motion_z), albedo, f0, and three demodulated 1-sample signals with hit distances: diffuse (cosine-sampled one-bounce GI), specular (GGX VNDF, divided by Karis 2014's analytic env-BRDF) and shadows (one ray toward a sphere light, plus occluder distance). The reference panel is the same estimator with many samples per pixel.

URL options are documented at the top of src/main.ts; mode=eval writes JSON metrics to window.__rt and run-headless.mjs collects them.
